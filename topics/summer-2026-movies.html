The Man Who Invented Summer Blockbusters

It is impossible to discuss summer 2026 movies without acknowledging that Steven Spielberg invented the summer blockbuster. The entire concept of a massive, event-driven summer movie season traces back to one film and one filmmaker.

How Spielberg Created the Summer Movie Season

In the summer of 1975, Jaws became the first film to gross $100 million at the domestic box office. More importantly, it pioneered the wide-release strategy — opening simultaneously in hundreds of theaters instead of the traditional slow rollout. It was backed by a massive television advertising campaign, another first. The result was a cultural phenomenon that reshaped Hollywood forever.

Before Jaws, summer was considered a dumping ground for lesser films. After Jaws, every studio wanted a summer tentpole. The season Spielberg created in 1975 is the same season he returns to in 2026 — fifty-one years later — with Disclosure Day.
