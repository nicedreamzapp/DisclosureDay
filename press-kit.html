Movie Overview

Disclosure Day is the new science fiction thriller from Academy Award-winning director Steven Spielberg. Returning to the genre that defined his career, Spielberg crafts an epic story of first contact, paranoia, and humanity's reckoning with the unknown.

The film follows a Kansas City weather woman, played by Emily Blunt, who may be humanity's first point of contact with extraterrestrial intelligence. As the world spirals between wonder and fear, a journalist (Josh O'Connor) races to uncover a decades-long cover-up, while powerful figures (Colin Firth, Colman Domingo) move to control the narrative.

"The truth belongs to seven billion people."

Written by David Koepp (Jurassic Park, War of the Worlds), scored by John Williams, and produced by Kristie Macosko Krieger, Disclosure Day marks Spielberg's return to alien cinema nearly 50 years after Close Encounters of the Third Kind (1977).

Official Synopsis

From Academy Award-winning director Steven Spielberg comes Disclosure Day, a sweeping science fiction thriller about the moment everything changes.

When a Kansas City weather woman begins receiving signals she cannot explain, an unraveling chain of events forces the world to confront a truth that has been hidden for decades: we are not alone. As governments scramble to control the narrative, a journalist uncovers evidence of a vast cover-up, while ordinary people around the globe must reckon with a reality that rewrites everything they thought they knew.

Featuring a screenplay by David Koepp and a score by John Williams, Disclosure Day is a story about wonder, fear, and the question of what humanity does when the truth finally arrives. Releasing June 12, 2026 in theaters and IMAX worldwide.
