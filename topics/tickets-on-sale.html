Should You See Disclosure Day in IMAX?

Absolutely yes. Steven Spielberg shot Disclosure Day with IMAX specifically in mind. The expanded aspect ratio and massive screen are designed to immerse you in the film's first contact sequences. Beyond the visuals, John Williams' score - potentially his final for a Spielberg film - deserves the best sound system available. IMAX's 12-channel sound system will deliver the full emotional impact of the music.

If you're only going to see it once in theaters, make it IMAX. The difference in scale and sound between a standard screening and IMAX is dramatic for a film built around spectacle and wonder.
