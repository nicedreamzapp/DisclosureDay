Why Everyone Is Talking About It

The Disclosure Day trailer didn't just generate hype — it generated the right kind of hype. Here's why the conversation exploded:

Spielberg's First Alien Movie in 20+ Years The last time Spielberg directed a film about extraterrestrials was War of the Worlds in 2005. Before that, E.T. in 1982 and Close Encounters in 1977. This isn't just a movie — it's a master returning to the genre he defined.
The Close Encounters Spaceship Connection The trailer features a spaceship design that bears a striking resemblance to the mothership from Close Encounters of the Third Kind. Fans noticed immediately, and the speculation hasn't stopped since.
Emily Blunt's Alien Possession Scene The moment Blunt's meteorologist is overcome by an extraterrestrial force during a live broadcast became an instant viral clip. People are calling it one of the most chilling trailer moments in years.
"People Deserve to Know" The trailer's core message — that the truth about alien life belongs to everyone, not governments — resonated deeply. The line "The truth belongs to 7 billion people" has become a rallying cry across fan communities.

The Close Encounters Theory Explodes

Of all the reactions to the Super Bowl trailer, one theory dominated the conversation above all others: the Close Encounters connection.

The spaceship design in the Disclosure Day trailer is unmistakably similar to the iconic mothership from Close Encounters of the Third Kind (1977). The resemblance sent fan communities into overdrive. Within hours of the trailer airing, forums, subreddits, and film Twitter were flooded with side-by-side comparisons and frame-by-frame analyses.

The debate centers on three possibilities:

  • Shared Universe — Is Disclosure Day set in the same universe as Close Encounters? Could this be a continuation of that story, decades later?
  • Spiritual Sequel — Rather than a direct narrative link, is Spielberg creating a thematic companion piece? A film that asks "what happens after first contact becomes undeniable?"
  • Deliberate Homage — Spielberg referencing his own iconic work as a visual touchstone, connecting Disclosure Day to his legacy without a literal plot connection.

Whatever the answer, the ambiguity is part of the genius. Spielberg has given the internet exactly enough to theorize endlessly — and that's fueling the hype machine.

What the Critics Are Saying

The critical reaction to the Super Bowl trailer has been overwhelmingly positive, with a few notable takes standing out:

"Something feels off... but a new Spielberg is a major event." — World of Reel

World of Reel acknowledged an unusual tonal quality to the trailer — a sense that something beneath the surface isn't quite what it seems — while simultaneously recognizing that any new Spielberg film demands attention. That tension between unease and anticipation mirrors the film's own themes of disclosure and hidden truths.

Tom's Guide named the Disclosure Day spot one of the best Super Bowl trailers of 2026, praising its combination of spectacle and emotional weight. The trailer's ability to deliver massive sci-fi visuals while maintaining an intimate, character-driven core is something critics have highlighted as distinctly Spielbergian.

The consensus: even skeptics are paying attention. When Steven Spielberg returns to the genre he invented, the entire film world watches.
