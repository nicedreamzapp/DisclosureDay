Disclosure Day Super Bowl Trailer

Trailer Aired - Super Bowl LX
IT HAPPENED
Spielberg dropped the full Disclosure Day trailer during Super Bowl LX on February 9, 2026

The Full Trailer Is Here

Universal Pictures debuted the full Disclosure Day trailer during Super Bowl LX on February 9, 2026 — and it delivered. After the teaser dropped on December 16, 2025, fans had been speculating for weeks about whether Spielberg would use the Super Bowl stage. He did.

The trailer opens with Emily Blunt's Kansas City TV meteorologist delivering a live weather report before being suddenly overcome by a mysterious extraterrestrial force on air. From there, the footage escalates into a vision of global chaos as the world learns that aliens truly exist.

"If you found out we weren't alone, if someone showed you, proved it to you, would that frighten you? This summer, the truth belongs to seven billion people."

What the Trailer Reveals

The Aliens — First Look The Super Bowl trailer reveals the alien presence for the first time. The teaser only hinted at them; this trailer shows them.
Warships Over Cities Massive alien warships hover over cities in dramatic shots that recall classic Spielberg sci-fi imagery.
Close Encounters Connection The spaceship design bears a striking resemblance to the mothership in Close Encounters of the Third Kind — fueling fan theories about a shared universe.
Crop Circles Crop circles appear across farmland, adding another layer to the alien contact mystery.
High-Speed Chase An intense car chase alongside a speeding train suggests the film has major action set pieces alongside its sci-fi drama.
Emily Blunt's Possession Blunt's meteorologist is overcome by an alien force during a live broadcast — the central inciting incident of the film.
"People Deserve to Know" The trailer's closing message frames the core conflict: disclosure vs. cover-up. The truth belongs to everyone.

What This Means

The Super Bowl trailer confirms Disclosure Day is a full-scale alien contact epic. The Close Encounters-style spaceship design, combined with the crop circles and warships, suggests Spielberg is drawing from his entire sci-fi legacy. With John Williams scoring his 30th Spielberg feature and a June 12 release in IMAX, this is shaping up to be the biggest event film of summer 2026.

What We Still Don't Know

  • Colin Firth's full role — first look images showed him with electrodes on his head, but the trailer keeps his arc mysterious
  • Colman Domingo's character — he's praised the film's "heart" but his role remains under wraps
  • John Williams' score — still waiting to hear the actual Williams compositions in a trailer
  • The alien agenda — are they here to help, to warn, or to invade?
  • The "seven billion" tagline — what does global disclosure actually look like in this film?
